GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently Asked Questions

Useful information about booking, airport pickups, payments, luggage and private transfers with GKS TRANSFER.

Do you monitor flight delays?

Yes. For airport pickups, the flight is monitored through FlightRadar so we can follow changes to the arrival time.

How do I contact my driver?

Communication for your transfer can be arranged directly through WhatsApp or Viber.

How can I pay for my transfer?

Payment is available by cash or card/POS.

Is a deposit required?

A deposit is only required for long-distance journeys. When applicable, the deposit is 20% of the agreed transfer price.

What is the cancellation policy?

Cancellation is free of charge up to 3 hours before the scheduled pickup time.

Are child seats available?

Yes, a child seat can be provided upon prior arrangement. Please mention it when making your request.

How many passengers and bags can you accommodate?

The vehicle can accommodate up to 4 passengers, with luggage capacity for up to 4 large suitcases and 3 small bags.

Are transfers available 24/7?

Yes. Transfers are available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, by prior booking.

Where can GKS TRANSFER take me?

There is no fixed destination limit. Private transfers can be arranged to destinations across Greece, subject to booking and agreement.

Can I request a stop or waiting time during my journey?

Yes. Intermediate stops or waiting time can be arranged in advance according to your journey requirements.

How can I make a booking?

You can send a request through the website booking form or contact GKS TRANSFER by phone, WhatsApp, Viber or email. Your booking is finalized after direct confirmation from GKS TRANSFER.

How quickly will my booking request be answered?

GKS TRANSFER will contact you within 6 hours of your request to confirm the details and finalize the booking.

Which languages are available?

Service and communication are available in English and Greek.

How much does a private transfer cost?

The price depends on the route and the details of your booking. Contact GKS TRANSFER with your pickup point, destination, date and time to receive a price for your journey.
